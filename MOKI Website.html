MOKI is a creative design and product studio started by two co-founders with no budget, just skills, time and free tools.

We make original things first: website designs and components, UI/UX concepts, app interfaces, reusable widgets, posters, stickers, illustrations and book covers. Then we give them away, with clear credit and licenses.

Our look is playful, colourful and a little mischievous. Our plan is simple: create, publish, share, improve, repeat.

Long term, MOKI wants to grow from digital design into physical products and hardware.
